<p>Pilou asks one question at each dose: "Did you take your medication?"</p>
<ul>
<li>One or several medications, each with its own times, days (every day, some days or monthly) and optional end date</li>
<li>Medications due at the same time are grouped into one reminder</li>
<li>Silent notification, no sound or vibration</li>
<li>"Yes, I took it" button right in the notification, or in the app</li>
<li>If there is no answer after the chosen delay (up to 24 h), an SMS is sent automatically to a loved one. The alert can be turned off per medication.</li>
<li>History of recent doses</li>
</ul>
<p>No account, no internet access, no ads. Data stays on the phone.</p>
<p>The app needs permission to send SMS, which is its core feature. Messages go through the phone's own plan.</p>
